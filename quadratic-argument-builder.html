2 Count — the discriminant, then how many real solutions it implies.

3 Solve — as many root blanks as they just claimed there are.

4 Interpret — throw away what the situation forbids, and answer in words.

Notes

The count blank rewrites the rest of the question — claim one solution and only one root blank ever appears. Wrong counts still produce a complete, coherent argument that fails on Check, rather than a dead end.

The rejection step only exists on the two-solution branch, which is where it is the actual mathematical work. Nothing is asked twice.

The graph appears only at stage 4. Shown earlier it would give away the count; shown at the end it ties the rejected root to the shaded "before the throw" region.
