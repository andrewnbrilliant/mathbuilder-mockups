Shape of it

Two sentences, three blanks — reaches all four classifications with nothing redundant on any path.

Each sentence pairs an observation blank with a conclusion blank: the learner has to commit to the evidence before naming the relationship.

Blanks appear one at a time. Sentence 2 is chosen by the learner's answer to sentence 1 — so a wrong first step leads to a coherent but false argument, not a dead end.

The four labels are always the full set, so the conclusion is never a coin flip between two.
